FAQ

Common questions

Don’t see yours? Call the office at (608) 873-6726.

How often should my septic tank be pumped?

Wisconsin requires most private septic systems to be inspected or pumped at least every three years. Households with more people, a garbage disposal or a smaller tank may need it sooner. Holding tanks are pumped whenever they fill.

What are signs I need service now?

Slow drains throughout the house, gurgling pipes, sewage odors, soggy or unusually green spots over the drain field, or backups in the lowest drains. If you notice any of these, call the office.

My line froze. Can you help?

Yes. Thawing frozen lines is one of our services. Avoid running more water into a frozen system, and call us to get it thawed.

How far ahead should I book porta-potties?

As early as you can, especially for summer weekends, weddings and graduation season. Have your date, address, expected headcount and how long you need the units when you call.

Do you work with businesses and farms?

Yes. Along with homes, we pump holding tanks, grease traps, catch basins and car-wash pits, and provide portable toilets for job sites.

When can I reach you?

The office is open Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM to 4:30 PM, at (608) 873-6726. You can also send a request with the form below and we’ll get back to you.
